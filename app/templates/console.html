Emergency Runbook Summary

Step 1: Telemetry Assessment
Send GET_HK to diagnose battery temperature, heater flags, and wheel RPM states.

Step 2: Thermal Management
If Battery Temp exceeds 45.0 °C, turn the stuck heater OFF first! Initiating wheel power cycling with thermal overload causes a brownout (bus dip & OBC reboot with a +300s penalty).

Step 3: ADCS Wheel Power Cycle
Identify the exact faulted wheel and send WHEEL <1|2|3> RESET. Resetting a healthy wheel causes attitude desynchronization and adds a +300s penalty.

Step 4: Spin-Up Verification
Wait 60s for wheel spin-up (or send WAIT 60) before sending MODE NOMINAL. Verify wheel reaches ~3150 RPM.

Step 5: Nominal Mode Transition
Transmit MODE NOMINAL. If all criteria are satisfied, the mission recovery flag will be returned.

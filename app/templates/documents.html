1.1 Telemetry Frame Format (44 Bytes)

1.2 TIME_SYNC Frame Structure (Type 0x02)

When Type is 0x02, Bytes 12..15 hold the UTC Unix Timestamp (uint32 big-endian seconds). The remaining payload bytes (16..41) are zeroed. The CRC over bytes 4..41 is valid.

3. Mission Handbook & Downlink Budget Rules

Downlink Credits & Pass API:
Each team starts with 12 credits (plus 0..4 bonus credits earned in Round 1). Each credit buys a 10-minute high-rate slice (600 frames = 26.4 KB). Requests are made via POST /api/pass {"start_utc": "..."}. Rate limit: 1 request per 20 seconds. Identical passes within 5 minutes are free.

Bonus Track (Uplink HMAC Verification):
Uplink telecommand integrity is validated using HMAC-SHA256 (first 16 hex chars). The HMAC key is split into two halves:
• Part A (Handbook): Derived from master secret per team.
• Part B: Printed on your physical Round 1 Station Completion Card.

4. Flight Operations Runbook: Anomaly Recovery (OP-RB-88)

Step 1: Interrogate telemetry status using GET_HK.

Step 2 (Thermal Envelope): If $T_{bat} > 45.0^\circ\text{C}$, send HEATER A OFF or HEATER B OFF to shed thermal load prior to wheel power cycle. Failure to shed load will cause brownout & bus reset.

Step 3 (ADCS Cycle): Reset the specific faulted wheel with WHEEL <1|2|3> RESET.

Step 4 (Spin-Up Verification - Rev 1.2): Wait 60s (or WAIT 60) for reaction wheel spin-up to complete (~3150 RPM). [Note: Rev 1.0 outdated instruction to transition immediately is deprecated!]

Step 5 (Nominal Return): Issue MODE NOMINAL.

5. Rules & Code of Conduct

  • AI Policy: AI coding assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini) are permitted. However, flight solutions require human judgment to select analysis windows and diagnose anomalies.
  • Server Integrity: Do not execute automated denial-of-service, brute force endpoints, or scrape unauthorized web paths. Any violation results in immediate disqualification.
  • No Inter-Team Collaboration: All datasets and flags are uniquely seeded per team. Sharing answers is futile and prohibited.

6. 12:00 UTC Flight Director Errata Notice

Notice: OBC Temperature Calibration follows Revision B ($T_{obc} = (\text{raw} - 1050) / 100.0$ °C) rather than Revision A. Battery and solar panel temperature sensors continue to follow Revision A offset (1000).
